# Web4 Whitepaper

## A Peer-to-Peer Money and Transaction System Without Intermediaries

### Transparent, Zero-Fee, and Absolutely Neutral

**Author: Neutral Absolute (a pseudonym)**

---

A purely peer-to-peer version of money would allow value to be sent directly from one person to another without going through a financial institution — and, crucially, without ever having to leave the system in order to be spent. Today's blockchain networks fail that test: value enters through a centralised exchange (CEX), circulates briefly, and exits back to a bank to be cashed out, paying fees, spreads and commissions at every step.

Web4 proposes a system in which the crypto balance is itself the payment instrument. Its value is expressed against the value of the coin and the currency of each user's own country, its ledger is transparent and publicly auditable, no party stands between sender and receiver, and the technology takes not a single unit of value from any transaction: **the fee is zero.** The network is maintained not by fees but by use itself — every transaction is energy that strengthens the technology — while per-person transaction limits, bound to a verified identity, replace fees as the defence against spam.

Web4 also corrects the founding generation. Satoshi Nakamoto's vision was noble, but unlimited anonymity became a shelter for money laundering and financial crime. Web4 therefore registers every user with an identity (ID) approved by their own state, while denying every official a blocking button: blocking is executed automatically by the system, only under clear rules. Rule changes are locked by absolute neutrality, taxation runs over the same transparent ledger, and lending is interest-free — large balance holders are obliged by the system to lend to people in hardship, under strict debt rules, verified hardship protection, and a single honour for the lender whose loss settles a sick borrower's debt: the DONATE label.

## The Ten Principles of Absolute Neutrality

1. Transactions run directly peer-to-peer; the crypto balance is used and settled through the balance itself, never looping back through a CEX and a bank.
2. Value adjusts to the value of the coin and to each country's own currency; the coin and its records are transparent and publicly auditable.
3. There is no middleman, and the technology takes nothing from any transaction: the fee is zero.
4. Every transaction is energy that strengthens the technology; the more it is used, the better it is maintained. The number of transactions per person is limited.
5. Users are registered with an ID and the approval of their own state, to prevent money laundering and crime.
6. Blocking is performed only by the system, under the rules — never directly by a government or an official.
7. Rules may be changed by the state only when the old rule burdens many parties and the change is absolutely neutral; new rules may be added only while they remain in line with the technology's vision and mission.
8. Web4 is a bank and a transaction instrument at once, transparently; tax is collected through the blockchain so that state and people are equally safe and equally benefited.
9. Loans carry no interest and no profit-sharing; large balance holders are obliged to lend to those in hardship, with strict rules for those who flee and protection for those struck by genuine hardship.
10. The DONATE label grants honour only. Before the rules, everyone is equal — no priority, no immunity, no exceptions.
